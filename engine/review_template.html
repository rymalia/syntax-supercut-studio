Speaker review: on camera or voice-over?

Each clip plays one segment the detector counted as spoken by the face on screen. The yellow box is the face it thinks is talking. Everything starts as Speaking, so you only need to mark the exceptions: Voice-over means the voice isn't that face (narration, an interviewer, someone off screen), and Mixed means only part of it is. Click Save decisions when you're done. It downloads decisions.json and your choices are also kept in this page.
